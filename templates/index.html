Project Purpose

This IoT project is designed to protect tray return areas by detecting and deterring birds. An Arduino equipped with an ultrasonic sensor is deployed at the tray return area to monitor bird presence, and a buzzer/audio player is activated to scare them away when they approach too close. Critical parameters—such as sensor range, audio selection, and volume—are transmitted to a remotely located Raspberry Pi, allowing adjustments through our web dashboard. Enhancements include a camera connected to the Pi for enhanced detection and distance calculation, periodic data logging into a database, and notifications sent to staff’s phones during periods of frequent bird activity to prompt tray clearing.

How It Works

The Arduino at the tray return area continuously monitors for birds using an ultrasonic sensor. A camera connected to the Raspberry Pi further enhances detection by measuring the distance between the birds and the tray area.

When birds come within a critical range, the Arduino activates a buzzer/audio player to scare them away. Sensor range, audio choice, and volume settings are remotely adjustable via the Raspberry Pi to ensure optimal deterrence.

Data on bird presence is sent from the Arduino to the Raspberry Pi and stored in a database. This real-time information is displayed on a web dashboard. For periods with frequent bird approaches, a notification is automatically sent to the staff’s phone, prompting timely tray clearing.

Ultrasonic Sensor Details

Our ultrasonic sensor measures the distance to birds by emitting a short, high-frequency sound and timing how long it takes to bounce back. This allows precise detection without harming or startling the birds. Adjust the threshold and distance to suit your specific environment.

Buzzer Frequency Details

Birds are known to be disturbed (without being harmed) by frequencies between 15 to 25 kHz. Thus, choosing 0% here corresponds to 15kHz while 100% would mean 25kHz. (Ignore the fact that the current buzzer could not output this.)
